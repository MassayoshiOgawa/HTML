HOW TO PLAY

Objective

Survive as long as possible. Red projectiles fill the arena — avoid them all. You have no weapons, only your reflexes.

Controls

W A S D or ↑ ← ↓ → — Move

ESC — Pause

Difficulty

Difficulty rises over time. More projectiles appear, moving faster with increasingly complex patterns. The longer you survive, the more chaotic it becomes.

Tips

Your hitbox is smaller than your sprite — use this to your advantage. Watch for gaps in patterns. Don't panic — find the rhythm.
